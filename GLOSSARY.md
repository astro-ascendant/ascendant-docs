# Ascendant

The sidereal astrology domain of the Ascendant site: a birth moment and place become a horoscope, read through Parashari, Jaimini, and KP methods.

## Language

### Position

**Longitude**:
An absolute sidereal position on the zodiac, measured in degrees from 0° Aries and always in the range 0–360.
_Avoid_: Angle, degree, absolute degree

**Sign degree**:
A position inside one sign, from 0° up to but not including 30°, shown in degrees and arcminutes.
_Avoid_: Degree, longitude

**Sign**:
One of the twelve 30° sidereal sectors, from Aries to Pisces.
_Avoid_: Rashi, except in "Rashi chart"

**Cusp**:
The longitude at which a house begins.
_Avoid_: House degree, house start

**House**:
One of the twelve divisions of a chart, counted from the lagna.
_Avoid_: Bhava, except when quoting a classical text

**Lagna**:
The point of the zodiac rising at the birth moment; it begins the first house.
_Avoid_: Rising sign, when the degree matters

**Nakshatra**:
A 13°20' lunar mansion, one of twenty-seven; each is divided into four pada.
_Avoid_: Star, lunar mansion

**Pada**:
One quarter of a nakshatra, 3°20' wide.
_Avoid_: Quarter, step

### Chart

**Placement**:
One body's position in a chart: sign, longitude, house, nakshatra, pada, and state.
_Avoid_: Planet row, entry

**Division**:
A divisional chart projected from the birth chart; D1 is the Rashi chart and D9 the Navamsha.
_Avoid_: Varga (unless quoting a classical text), sub-chart

**Dasha**:
A timed planetary period system. Vimshottari, Chara, and Sthira are the three the site reads.
_Avoid_: Period, when the system is meant

**Ayanamsa**:
The offset between the tropical and sidereal zodiacs; the site reports the one each calculation used.
_Avoid_: Zodiac offset

**State**:
A placement's retrograde flag or dignity, as shown in the placement table.
_Avoid_: Status, condition

### KP

**KP sub-lord**:
The Vimshottari subdivision of a nakshatra; the sub-sub-lord is the next level down.
_Avoid_: Sub, sub lord
